▸ CONTROLS

MOVE CARDS

  • Drag a card sideways in your hand to reorder it. The neighbours part to show where it lands.
  • Drag a card up to the table, or tap it on your turn, to play or discard it.
  • In Sandbox, drag cards anywhere. Double-click, or press F while hovering, to flip one.
▸ GIN RUMMY

MELD YOUR TEN

  • Build sets (3–4 of a rank, green ring) and runs (3+ in a row, same suit, cyan ring).
  • Each turn, draw one card from the stock or the discard pile, then discard one.
  • Knock when your unmatched deadwood is 10 or less. Hit 0 for Gin and a 25-point bonus.
▸ HEARTS

DODGE THE POINTS

  • Each heart costs 1 point and the Queen of spades costs 13. Take all 26 to shoot the moon.
  • Pass 3 cards left. The 2 of clubs leads the first trick; follow suit when you can.
